Part of our mission is getting young people to think about the environment and their role in protecting it. Schools are the most direct way for us to reach them.

On 9 March 2022, our team visited MILS school to talk with students about environmental protection and sustainability.

We brought our eco-friendly car and explained how we design it to use as little energy as possible, and why those choices matter. We also talked with the students about university majors, and how environmental protection is becoming part of many fields of study.

Then we told them about our journey to the Shell Eco-Marathon: the months of preparation, the competition itself, and the rankings we earned against teams from around the world.

The students asked plenty of questions and showed real interest. Their response encouraged us to plan more visits to schools, fairs, and other events where we can reach young people. We want to help raise a generation that understands how its everyday choices affect the environment.
