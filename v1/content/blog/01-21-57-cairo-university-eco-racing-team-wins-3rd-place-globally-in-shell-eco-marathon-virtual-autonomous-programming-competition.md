Cairo University Eco-Racing Team placed **3rd globally** in the **Shell Eco-Marathon Autonomous Programming Competition**, one of the toughest international challenges in autonomous vehicle programming.

Over several months, our team designed the algorithms, researched different approaches, and tested the car's autonomous decision-making again and again until it held up. The result puts Cairo University students, and Egyptian engineering, on an international podium.

What this result means for us:

- Egyptian students can compete with the strongest teams in the world.
- Autonomous technology will be central to the future of mobility, and we want to be part of building it.
- It pushes us to keep growing our technical skills in innovation and sustainability.

Thank you to every team member, mentor, and supporter who made this possible.

We're already preparing for the next competition. Follow our social media pages for updates.
