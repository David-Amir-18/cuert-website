The Shell Eco-Marathon Egypt local event brings together student teams who design and build their own energy-efficient vehicles. Teams compete to cover the most distance on the least fuel, and they also present and pitch their work to judges.

Our team won **$5,000 in prize money and equipment worth $1,000**. We also earned one of the highest rankings in technical inspection and in pitching, the result of a full year of work on the car and on our presentation.

The event also gave us time with the other teams. We traded ideas, compared approaches, and learned from their experience, and we'll carry those lessons into our next car.

We're proud of this result, and we're aiming higher next year.
